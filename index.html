The Project

A pixel-art experiment that became a DAO

Sept 2021

Genesis mint on Arbitrum

2,000 generative pixel-robot avatars mint out, rendered entirely by the smart contract — no external image hosting required.

2021–2022

Extensions ship

The community builds BotCases and BotPets, expanding what a Bot can hold and how it's displayed.

BotDAO forms

1 Bot = 1 vote

Holders take the wheel. Treasury and roadmap decisions move to on-chain governance.

Ongoing

CC0 release

Arbibots art and IP are released CC0 — free for anyone to build on, remix, or commercialize.
